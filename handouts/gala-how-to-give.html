Every gift, ticket, sponsorship, and auction item goes through one online form on our gala page. It takes about a minute, and it makes sure every supporter gets a thank-you note and a receipt.

For donors & guestsHow to give

  1. Scan the code or go to fcrcrowsneck.org/gala, then tap the button for the form.
  2. Enter your name, email, and mailing address so we can send your thank-you and receipt.
  3. Fill in any that apply. One payment can be split across these:
    Donation · a general gift
    Tickets · $50 each
    Sponsorship · business or family sponsor
    Auction item I won · paying for an item you won
  4. Donating an item to the auction? Check the box, then describe the item and give its estimated value.
  5. Choose how you'll pay (Pay online now, Check, or Cash), then tap Submit.
Paying online? After you submit, tap Pay now and enter the amount shown. You can use a debit or credit card, PayPal, or Venmo. You don't need a PayPal account.

For volunteersHelping someone give

Email is required. The form won't submit without one, and there's no way around it. Ask first.

Always get a full mailing address. Karen uses it to mail thank-you notes and donation receipts.

  • Use the form for every gift, ticket, sponsorship, and auction item, not paper, so nothing gets missed.
  • Pull up fcrcrowsneck.org/gala on your phone, or have guests scan the code and fill it out themselves.
  • Won an item and donating an item are different: "Auction item I won" is for paying for a win, and the checkbox is for giving an item to the auction.
  • For card, PayPal, or Venmo, have them choose Pay online now on their own phone. Enter each gift only once.
  • Wait for the "Thank you!" screen before moving on.
  • If the form gives you trouble, write the details down and let Tamara know. We'll enter it later.
